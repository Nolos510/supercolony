# Specimens, regional context, and dated research maps

Reviewed **2026-10-13**. This bundled source pack supplies the approved mobile/learning/recognition revision. It does not monitor current colony boundaries or publish a live battlefield. Metadata lives in `src/data/research.ts`, `src/data/regional.ts`, and `src/data/specimens.ts`.

## Research data

- **2010 global study:** [Van Wilgenburg, Torres & Tsutsui](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3352483/), observations from 2008–2009. `public/research/global-2010.json` includes nine samples and exactly 17 explicit pairwise aggression results: seven with none, two described as low, eight described as high. These categories follow the paper’s Results wording, not a new threshold. Unknown pairs remain unknown; no transitive tolerance links are inferred. Local game cooperation is a separate abstraction.
- **Coordinates:** five global markers convert the printed Table 1 coordinates. Marseille, Melbourne, Wellington, and Stellenbosch use disclosed approximate locality centers because the table contains invalid seconds/minutes, inconsistent hemispheres, or duplicated California coordinates. The Lake Hodges marker preserves the printed coordinate and discloses the table’s additional “La Mesa” label. Markers cannot establish nest boundaries.
- **2025 California resurvey:** [Hathaway, Menke & Holway](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10530-025-03583-8), original surveys 1995–1997, resurveys 2022–2024. The official publisher’s [Appendix 1](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs10530-025-03583-8/MediaObjects/10530_2025_3583_MOESM1_ESM.xlsx) and [Appendix 2](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs10530-025-03583-8/MediaObjects/10530_2025_3583_MOESM2_ESM.xlsx) provide 295 unique GPS sites: 111 Torrey Pines and 184 Point Loma. Separating historical records yields 406 observation rows: 111 original and 295 resurvey rows. Missing historical values are omitted. The article is [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); authors and transformations are credited.
- **Method limits:** the presence column combines available resurvey evidence; it cannot be separated into method-specific detection counts. Original and resurvey pitfall designs differ. “Not detected” is used in place of proven absence. Exact individual visit dates and GPS error are not supplied. Figure 2’s caption states 80 additional Point Loma sites, while Methods and Appendix 2 list 168; this pack follows all 168 appendix rows and discloses the inconsistency.

`public/research/provenance.json` records original XLSX SHA-256 hashes, direct destinations, counts, transformations, and uncertainty. Coordinates are rounded to six decimal places without claiming that level of field accuracy. Source QA found one clearly misplaced positive longitude in Appendix 1: sampling point “49 - Guy Flemming” is named at Torrey Pines but printed at +117.257748. Both period rows preserve `reportedLongitude` and render an explicitly approximate west-sign correction at −117.257748 with a visible `coordinateNote`. No other coordinates were corrected. The game draws its own geometry; no author-created research map figure is copied. Neither dataset establishes current alliances, frontlines, or a census.

## Regional facts

Each of eight contexts contains environment, ecological stakes, an explicitly simulated game interpretation, and source links. The existing catalog IDs remain valid; additional regional evidence is linked directly.

| Region | Supporting evidence |
|---|---|
| California | [2025 resurvey](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10530-025-03583-8): moisture/soil associations and native-ant richness at two coastal protected areas. |
| Mediterranean | [Giraud, Pedersen & Keller (2002)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC122904/): historical recognition distinctions among sampled southern European populations. |
| Japan | [Seko and colleagues (2021)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7859199/): four Kobe Port supercolonies, sampled 2017–2018, with differing diet breadth. Association does not demonstrate a causal explanation for invasion success. |
| Southern Africa | [Bond & Slingsby (1984)](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2307/1938311): disrupted seed transport and burial in Cape fynbos. The paper’s tested plant relationship is not generalized into a numerical game prediction. |
| Australia | [Lymbery, Webber & Didham (2023)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10500280/): controlled terrain/worker combat trials. Game coefficients remain balanced values. |
| New Zealand | [Department of Conservation](https://www.doc.govt.nz/nature/pests-and-threats/animal-pests-and-threats/argentine-ants/): connected nests and checking movable plants, soil, materials and gear. |
| Hawaiʻi | [NPS insects and arthropods](https://www.nps.gov/hale/learn/nature/insects-arachnids-arthropods.htm), [Cole and colleagues (1992), primary publisher](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2307/1940678): endemic invertebrates in Haleakalā’s high-elevation shrublands. Hawaiʻi has no native ants; Ecologist’s label is native habitat/invertebrate communities. Maui evidence is distinguished from the fictional Hawaiʻi Island anchor. |
| South America | [Wild (2004)](https://academic.oup.com/aesa/article/97/6/1204/54982): native Paraná drainage and distinction from introduced South American populations. Native-range context receives no Ecologist invasion pressure. |

PMC and AntWeb sometimes return access challenges to direct retrieval. Indexed primary content supported the regional review; publication identities were checked. Official destinations are retained rather than presenting a mirror as the biological authority.

## Worker photographs and external 3D viewer

`public/specimens/credits.json` contains author, identity, original source, archive source, rights evidence, dimensions, checksum, and changes for each delivered photograph. Both original JPEGs are retained unchanged.

- **Argentine worker:** CASENT0006019, photographed by **April Nobile**, from **www.antweb.org**. [AntWeb’s individual photograph page](https://www.antweb.org/bigPicture.do?name=casent0006019&number=1&shot=p) names the photographer; the [specimen record](https://www.antweb.org/specimen.do?code=casent0006019) identifies a worker. The unchanged [archived JPEG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Linepithema_humile_casent0006019_profile_1.jpg) carries an explicit original **CC BY-SA 3.0** grant in its copyright metadata. This pack follows that preserved grant, despite the archive’s newer generic CC BY 4.0 labeling. Display credit, specimen code, original source link and [license link](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) accompany it; any future photographic adaptations must respect share-alike terms. This license applies to the photograph, not an assertion that the whole game uses that license.
- **Meat-ant workers:** **Steve Shattuck / CSIRO**, ScienceImage 3225. The [archived file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:CSIRO_ScienceImage_3225_Meat_ant_Iridomyrmex_purpureus_Formicidae.jpg) identifies the species, author, and **CC BY 3.0**; original EXIF retains the CSIRO credit. [CSIRO’s official 31 July 2023 collection statement](https://www.csiro.au/en/news/All/News/2023/July/ScienceImage-Online) grants Creative Commons Attribution reuse of ScienceImage. The old ScienceImage host did not resolve on review. The unchanged archive bytes preserve the photograph, with visible photographer/CSIRO credit and [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
- **Antscan:** [Specimen 1040](https://biomedisa.info/antscan/specimen/1040/) identifies **Linepithema humile, worker, OKENT0105450**. It is a different specimen from the photograph. The site lists processed scan/model data; the game only links to its viewer and redistributes no scan or model. No exact meat-ant 3D scan is asserted.

CASENT0106119 and CASENT0010657 photographs were not bundled because individual acquisition/license verification was unavailable within the source pass; independently verified alternatives satisfy the approved species-photograph requirement. No noncommercial-only image is included. Anatomy diagrams are original illustrations and photos are not shown at a common scale. Listings and credits imply no partnership or endorsement.

## Freeze and maintenance

Data and assets were acquired from the approved sources without installing libraries, contacting authors, or scraping during runtime. A stdlib XLSX conversion checked IDs, coordinate bounds, point counts, and historical missing values. Before public release, recheck the official destinations and update the editorial review date when content changes. Keep source and license evidence alongside any asset replacement.
