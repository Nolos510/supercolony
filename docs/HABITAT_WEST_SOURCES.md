## Acquisition and transformations

Each individual file page was read before acquisition to check its description, real locality, creator, and reuse grant. Own-work declarations support the photographer credits. The Fort Ord photo credits Jesse Pluim / BLM and has the federal BLM public-domain template plus a reviewed institutional Flickr source. The two Cape photographs have the photographer’s explicit worldwide public-domain dedication. Creative Commons derivatives retain their listed image licenses; these licenses apply to the photograph assets, not to unrelated game code.

Five files were acquired as official Commons 960px JPEG thumbnails because the full-resolution server returned HTTP 429 and explicitly directed acquisition to standard thumbnails. `originalSha256` records the exact acquired source JPEG for each image; `sourceAcquisition`, `imageUrl`, and `changes` distinguish thumbnails from full-resolution source files. `originalImageUrl` retains the full-resolution destination without claiming its bytes were acquired. Three full-resolution originals were acquired: the Fort Ord coastal oak, the Calanques view, and the lower Paraná vegetation photograph.

Final assets use proportional resizing only where the source exceeds a 1200px long edge, JPEG quality 78, and no crop, retouch, or scene alteration. The manifest records acquired/final dimensions, acquisition SHA-256, final SHA-256, and the reviewed source-page SHA-256. Original acquired JPEGs and source HTML are retained locally in the ignored `output/habitat-preflight-west/` archive. Runtime uses bundled files and metadata only.

## Regional interpretation

The California grassland is in Santa Clarita Valley and the oak photo is at coastal Fort Ord; neither is presented as the San Diego resurvey plot. Mediterranean examples span Marseille’s exposed limestone coast and inland Mediterranean woodland in Montes de Toledo. Cape images distinguish flowering fynbos slopes from a wooded trail where native fynbos meets a pine plantation; the latter is not described as intact native forest. The Paraná examples are a sandy/grassy bank in Corrientes Province and dense creek-edge vegetation in the lower delta in Buenos Aires Province, both explicitly labeled with their own localities rather than the fictional Corrientes gateway.

The Paraná open image includes a recreation area and the complex image shows stream-edge vegetation. The photos describe visible surroundings, not where ants were sampled. The individual source captions and reviewed pages, rather than generic regional assumptions, establish locality.

## Freeze

All eight final JPEGs and `public/habitats/west/credits.json` passed existence, pair uniqueness, dimensions, date, and SHA-256 checks. Each selected image was visually inspected. No unknown-license, noncommercial-only, generated, or placeholder asset is included.
