# Western atlas habitat source pack

Reviewed 2026-10-14. Twenty-four distinct real photographs resolve six fictional setting roles in each of California, Mediterranean Europe, South Africa, and the Paraná basin. The manifest is `public/habitats/atlas-west/credits.json`; each assignment is keyed by `scenario: regional`, region ID, and role ID. Ecologist reuses the same regional settings.

Every image must display **Representative habitat; the game nest is fictional.** Real surroundings, creator, and locality are documented; photographs do not establish ant occurrence, sampled colonies, alliances, transport events, or current battlefronts. Terrain classifications describe the scenario rather than measurements of these photographed locations.

## Acquisitions and transformations

Eighteen new photographs were acquired using the official published Commons rendition URL returned by image metadata; six were copied unchanged from the prior individually verified western bundle after fresh source and role-fit review. A rendition’s `originalSha256` identifies its exact acquired bytes, not an unfetched maximum-resolution source. The six reused copies retain their prior acquired-source hash and transformation history, with a `previousBundleId` explaining reuse. Existing photographs and credit manifests were not modified.

New images were proportionally limited to a 1200-pixel long edge, converted to JPEG at quality 80, and normalized for EXIF orientation. No crop, retouch, synthetic addition, watermark removal, or scene alteration was performed. Existing copies are byte-identical to their prior bundled photographs. Final/acquired dimensions, acquisition URL, SHA-256, source revision, and review date are recorded per image. Preserve complete compositions, including portrait scenes; use containment rather than silently cropping the source.

Local preflight source revisions, source metadata, acquired JPEGs, and visual review sheets are retained in the integration-owner evidence archive; the public manifest contains direct permanent evidence URLs and hashes. No runtime scraping is needed.

## Setting fit and exclusions

Six primaries per region have distinct file identities and distinct final hashes. Gateway photographs show real transport infrastructure or waterside access and only illustrate the scenario connector. Garden edges show maintained planting or lawn boundaries. Open examples show exposed ground; grove examples emphasize tree shelter. Leaf-litter roles use ground cover and woody debris as representative obstacles; they do not claim that the photographed frame is a mapped trail. Habitat margins show transitions between physical surroundings.

Rejected candidates included a Marseille former bear enclosure (poor garden-edge fit), an exposed California hillside proposed for litter, and a broad rural Viale road (weaker litter fit and only approximate locality). The delivered Paraná litter example instead shows dense Predelta understory and fallen woody debris with the collection’s explicit locality and permission evidence.
