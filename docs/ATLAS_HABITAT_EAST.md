# Eastern habitat atlas: reviewed source and asset pack

Reviewed **2026-10-14**. This pack supplies **31 distinct real photographs** for **36 setting assignments**: six settings in each of Japan, Hawaiʻi, New Zealand and Australia, plus all twelve Australian Species Clash roles. Eight previously bundled photographs were individually rechecked and copied without further image changes. Twenty-three photographs were acquired for this revision.

The game nests are fictional. These photographs illustrate physical surroundings; they do not establish Argentine-ant occurrence, colony identity, current frontlines, measured combat outcomes or the locality of a simulated nest. Open/complex labels describe the game’s terrain abstraction. A real landscape can contain both conditions. Gateway examples show human access or transport infrastructure, rather than verified ant gateway nests.

## Locality and interpretation limits

- Kobe is an aerial port view. Whangārei is a marina; Lower Kahuku is a visitor station and access road. These are contextual human transport edges, not photographed ant transport events.
- The Port Adelaide image was taken in **2006**. Its date is retained in metadata and its caption identifies a historical view; it is not a claim about current waterfront infrastructure.
- The forest-trail source gives Kīlauea, Hawaiʻi Island, without an exact trail name. The dry-creek source says near Adelaide. The Victorian forest-floor source supplies Victoria without a town. Those limits are preserved rather than replaced with invented coordinates.
- The two Bidgee photographs are different frames of the **same Wagga Wagga field setting**. Their individual sources confirm an approximate locality and intentionally withhold a private-property position. No precise coordinates or camera metadata are bundled. The source identifies photographed meat ants; that does not identify the species occupying a fictional game nest after ownership changes.
- The New Zealand margin image shows the Te Whara / Bream Head forested headland and exposed coastal surroundings. It illustrates a landscape transition; the open game category is not a measured description of every pixel.
- Lower Kahuku’s ʻōhiʻa/lava example supports Hawaiʻi’s native plant and invertebrate habitat framing. No native-ant community is claimed.

## Rights and image handling

All new Commons images were checked on their individual file pages for creator, locality and the explicit image license. Generic Commons footer licenses were not used as image permission. When a file offers several licenses, the selected CC license in credits.json governs the bundled derivative. Share-alike photos retain the corresponding selected share-alike license; this does not relabel the application’s source-code license.

The two DOC images were rechecked on their individual page for the actual hero image and Image: DOC credit. The [DOC copyright policy](https://www.doc.govt.nz/footer-links/copyright/) allows commercial reuse of DOC-captioned photographs under CC BY 4.0; third-party images are excluded. Attribute **Department of Conservation (NZ)** outside New Zealand. Original individual source and policy links remain in credits.json.

Each selected NPS photograph was checked against its official NPGallery asset record, which explicitly records **Public domain** and **Full** rights. NPS credit remains visible metadata. Include **No claim to original U.S. Government works.** with republished NPS photographs, following the [NPS ownership notice](https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm#Ownership). This pack makes no agency partnership or endorsement claim.

The Waipoua kauri image has Szilas’s explicit public-domain dedication on its individual file page. It is not assigned CC0 from a generic footer. The institutional DOI / Neal Herbert forest-floor image uses the file’s verified **CC BY-SA 2.0** grant; government authorship alone is not treated as proof of public-domain status.

New image processing applied EXIF orientation, converted to RGB JPEG at quality 90, and resized proportionally to a maximum 1200-pixel edge. No photo was cropped, retouched, composited or generated. The Lower Kahuku station original was PNG, converted to JPEG; other local conversions are disclosed per asset. The eight reused files preserve their earlier resize/conversion description and add a statement that this revision made no further edits.

Metadata records the exact downloaded rendition URL, originalSha256 before local processing, bundled sha256, decoded dimensions, source dimensions for new acquisitions, selected license, source evidence, creator, locality, changes and review date. Original hash means the downloaded Wikimedia rendition, rather than a separately downloaded maximum-resolution master; NPS downloads used the official original asset. Processing strips inherited EXIF from new JPEG files. Review date is not an observation or capture date.

## Preflight verification

- All **31 JPEGs** decoded successfully and matched their recorded dimensions and bundled SHA-256 hashes.
- All **31 IDs, source URLs and bundled hashes** are distinct. All photographs are referenced by at least one assignment.
- The **36 assignments** resolve to existing typed role IDs: 24 regional assignments (six in each of four regions) and twelve Species Clash assignments. The scenario discriminator avoids leaf-litter role collisions.
- All source, rendition, rights and license links are HTTPS; every record is reviewed 2026-10-14. Every downloaded asset has a 64-character original SHA-256.
- The final set was visually reviewed on four contact sheets, with replacement dune and leaf-covered-path photographs also inspected individually. Total bundled image bytes: **13,783,382**.
- Rejected candidates: a close-up tourist boat failed the gateway setting; red foliage alone failed the litter setting; a night garden lacked useful habitat context; a water-dominated Coorong estuary failed sandy-clearing fit; one mound photograph lacked individually supported locality. None of those rejected images is delivered.

The pixel/rights preflight has no unresolved asset delivery gaps. Integration, rendered captions, layout, production build and phone playtesting belong to the integration owner’s protected completion checks. This preflight did not run npm, change feature code, install dependencies, commit or push.
