# Eastern habitat source pack

Reviewed 2026-10-13 (America/Los_Angeles). Eight real photographs: open and complex surroundings for Japan, southern Australia, northern New Zealand, and the Hawaiian islands. `public/habitats/east/credits.json` is the integration metadata and SHA-256 manifest. All eight downloaded images were visually inspected before this pack was frozen.

These photographs illustrate physical surroundings. They do not document an Argentine-ant or meat-ant occurrence, identify a supercolony, or measure a combat outcome. Their open/complex assignments are representative visual examples of the game's simplified terrain categories. Every displayed photograph must carry the label **Representative habitat; the game nest is fictional.** Locality and habitat are real; gateway and local nest positions remain simulated.

## Rights review and distribution

- Commons entries have individual author/own-work statements and explicit selected CC licenses. The individual licenses permit redistribution and adaptation with attribution and change disclosure. The resized photographs retain their stated licenses; CC BY-SA derivatives must remain under the corresponding compatible share-alike license. This does not replace the game's code license.
- [DOC's copyright policy](https://www.doc.govt.nz/footer-links/copyright/) specifically allows commercial reuse of photographs captioned DOC under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Both selected individual photographs carry that credit without a third-party copyright symbol. Unknown photographers are credited to the institution; no name is invented. Attribute as Department of Conservation (NZ) when used outside New Zealand. The two generic Northland images credited to third-party photographers were excluded.
- The [NPS gallery metadata endpoint](https://www.nps.gov/npgallery/api/search/execute/albumid/41CDBBEE-D6E1-44C0-AB53-5C069DF21CCB?pagesize=500&primarytype=image) gives **Public domain** and **Full** rights on both selected individual assets. Each photo has an NPS photographer credit without a copyright symbol. [NPS ownership guidance](https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm#Ownership) supports their reuse and asks commercial republication to acknowledge original government work: **No claim to original U.S. Government works.** No NPS logo or mark is included. Neither NPS nor DOC implies a partnership or endorsement.

## File provenance and edits

Every bundled JPEG was resized proportionally to 1200 pixels wide using macOS `sips`. No crop, retouch, synthetic addition, or content alteration was performed. The metadata records the exact download URL, the download's SHA-256 before resizing, and the bundled file's SHA-256 after resizing. `originalSha256` means the original **downloaded rendition**, not an independently fetched maximum-resolution original.

Wikimedia's maximum-resolution image service returned HTTP 429 and directed callers to supported thumbnail sizes. The four Commons assets therefore use the public, explicitly supported 1280px rendition from `thumb.wikimedia.org`, then resize to 1200px. The source identities and licenses remain the individually reviewed Commons file entries. DOC and NPS use published source JPEGs. No runtime scraping is required.

Visual notes: the Adelaide open photograph is a deliberately wide panorama (1200×348). Preserve its composition rather than presenting its crop as a different site. The Japan bamboo photograph is the author's disclosed focus stack; no AI/generated content is used. Hawaiʻi Island photographs are separate from the Maui/Haleakalā study context and must not be labeled as study sites.

Original downloads, source-page HTML, the NPS API response, and rights snapshots used during verification are retained in ignored `output/habitat-east-evidence/`; the distributable manifest contains direct evidence URLs and file hashes. Unresolved rights: none among these eight assets.
