## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create one production comic artwork sheet for SUPERColony, an educational ant strategy game, containing exactly FOUR equal panels in a clean 2 by 2 grid with thin warm-paper gutters. NO text, letters, speech bubbles, logos, interface, borders, or captions inside panels; narration is separate HTML. Original humorous field-atlas watercolor and ink illustration, tactile paper, muted moss terrain, warm ochre foliage, mineral-blue atmosphere, charcoal ink and small cyan/amber accents. Charming cinematic detail, readable at phone scale. Consistent central protagonist in all panels: General Mandible, a small brown Argentine-ant WORKER with six legs, two antennae, narrow single waist and gaster, a fanciful tiny bicorne hat and dark-teal sash; theatrical and pompous, no new biological soldier caste. This is explicitly comic fantasy. TOP LEFT: Paraná river floodplain origin, ant-scale plants, damp leaf litter, river glimmer far behind, General surveying ordinary busy workers and other insects. TOP RIGHT: accidental human transport, ants inside soil of a nursery plant pot, crates and shipping hold enormous at ant scale, General absurdly points toward the horizon from the rim, unaware a human hand carries the pot. BOTTOM LEFT: California-inspired dry grove/garden edge, General directs an epic tiny worker swarm marching along a trail and through a rock bottleneck, cavalry-style cinematic composition but actual ants walking, no horses, gore, firearms or humans fighting. BOTTOM RIGHT: recognition rivalry as imaginary rebellion, General peers at three separate opposing ant groups under amber cloth banners across a leaf-litter clearing; separated groups, no implied real rival alliance; exaggerated suspense and expressive body poses. Physical habitats illustrated, not photographs. Each panel has its own composition edge-to-edge, avoid important subjects at extreme edges. Four equally sized scenes, consistent protagonist and hand-painted atlas identity. Wide landscape sheet so each quarter reads as landscape, crisp high-quality art.
