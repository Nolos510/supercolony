# Specimen assets

Two unchanged archived scientific photographs are bundled. Individual source and license metadata are in `public/specimens/credits.json`; source review, image hashes, verification limitations, and the Antscan specimen are documented in [SOURCE_PACK_RECOGNITION.md](SOURCE_PACK_RECOGNITION.md).

- Argentine ant worker CASENT0006019: April Nobile, from AntWeb; original embedded CC BY-SA 3.0 license retained. The photograph and any future adaptations retain that license.
- Meat-ant workers, CSIRO ScienceImage 3225: Steve Shattuck / CSIRO; CC BY 3.0. The original ScienceImage host is unavailable; the owner’s collection-wide reuse grant and archived credit were checked.
- The Argentine-ant Antscan viewer shows a different micro-CT worker, OKENT0105450. No scan volume or 3D runtime is bundled.

Original anatomy schematic and tactical graphics remain original game assets. No photographs are AI-generated, and no author-created research map figure is copied.
